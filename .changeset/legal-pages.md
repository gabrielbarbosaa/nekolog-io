Publish the terms of use and the privacy policy at `/legal/terms` and `/legal/privacy`, in English, Portuguese and Spanish, linked from the home page. The documents are typed data rather than markup, so a parity test can fail when a translation drops a clause, lags behind on the date, leaves an entry blank or still carries the English sentence. The policy says plainly what is deliberately never stored — the IP address, the password, the API key — and who the data is processed with.
