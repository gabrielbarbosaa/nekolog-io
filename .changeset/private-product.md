The site and the documentation describe a private, paid product.

The landing page's "What is open and what is not" section became "What runs
where": the SDK goes into your application, everything behind it is operated
for you. The primary action is asking for an invitation, and the links to the
repository and to the licence are gone, because a private repository is a 404
for a visitor.

The documentation lost the self-host guide, which stopped being a feature when
customers stopped running the server, and 54 repeated "not on npm yet"
callouts across three languages. The introduction says what a customer gets
rather than where the open-core line was drawn.
